## Slide 2

Diagrama: plantilla completa del Business Model Canvas (9 bloques) aplicada a Starbucks, con el logo de la sirena de Starbucks (verde/morado) superpuesto de fondo, centrado sobre los bloques 2, 6 y 7. Cada uno de los 9 bloques del Canvas tiene un círculo morado numerado (1-9) y su texto guía en inglés (plantilla estándar Osterwalder), casi ilegible por el tamaño pequeño de fuente pero reconocible como el Canvas estándar:

| # | Bloque | Preguntas guía (texto pequeño, plantilla estándar) |
|---|--------|------|
| 8 | Key Partners | Who are our Key Partners? Who are our key suppliers? Which Key Resources are we acquiring from partners? Which Key Activities do partners perform? — Motivaciones: optimización y economía, reducción de riesgo/incertidumbre, adquisición de recursos/actividades particulares |
| 6 | Key Activities | What Key Activities do our Value Propositions require? Distribution Channels? Customer Relationships? Revenue streams? — Categorías: Production, Problem Solving, Platform/Network |
| 7 | Key Resources | What Key Resources do our Value Propositions require? — Tipos: Physical, Intellectual, Human, Financial |
| 2 | Value Propositions | What value do we deliver to the customer? Which problems are we helping to solve? — Características: Newness, Performance, Customization, Design, Brand/Status, Price, Cost Reduction, Risk Reduction, Accessibility, Convenience/Usability |
| 4 | Customer Relationships | What type of relationship does each Customer Segment expect? — Ejemplos: Personal assistance, Dedicated Personal Assistance, Self-Service, Automated Services, Communities, Co-creation |
| 3 | Channels | Through which Channels do our Customer Segments want to be reached? — Fases: Awareness, Evaluation, Purchase, Delivery, After sales |
| 1 | Customer Segments | For whom are we creating value? Who are our most important customers? — Mass Market, Niche Market, Segmented, Diversified, Multi-sided Platform |
| 9 | Cost Structure | What are the most important costs? Which Key Resources/Activities are most expensive? — Cost-driven vs Value-driven; Fixed costs, Variable costs, Economies of scale/scope |
| 5 | Revenue Streams | For what value are customers willing to pay? — Tipos: Asset sale, Usage fee, Subscription Fees, Lending/Renting/Leasing, Licensing, Brokerage fees, Advertising; Fixed pricing vs Dynamic pricing |

Esta slide es la plantilla de referencia genérica (en inglés) que se irá completando con contenido específico de Starbucks en las slides siguientes (build-up progresivo).
