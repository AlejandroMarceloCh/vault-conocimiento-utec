- **T (Technological)**: Technological access, Infrastructure, Research, Technology trends.
- **E (Environmental)**: Environmental policies, Recycling, Consumption trends, Production processes, Natural risks.
- **L (Legal)**: Wages, Rights, Job security, Regulations, Controlled of Marketing.
