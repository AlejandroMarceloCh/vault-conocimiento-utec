## Slide 7

Título: "1.1 COMPLIANCE RATE". Texto: "Contractual and policy compliance are pivotal to ensure legal security. If these compliance rates dip down, they can spike up indirect and maverick spend. ## Slide 8

Título: "1.2 SUPPLIER DEFECT RATE". Texto: "Supplier defect rate is used to evaluate a supplier's individual quality. Measuring supplier defect rates and breaking it down based on the defect type will offer actionable insights into a supplier's trustworthiness. Supplier defect rates are usually measured in defects per million."

Fórmula (viñeta amarilla):
$$\text{Supplier defect rate} = \frac{\text{Number of substandard products}}{\text{Total number of units tested}}$$

Foto decorativa a la derecha: dos trabajadores con cascos blancos inspeccionando una pared/techo con daños de humedad, sosteniendo una carpeta/portapapeles — simboliza inspección de calidad/defectos.

## Slide 9

Título: "1.3 PO ACCURACY". Texto: "Low PO accuracy hikes up operating costs. Procurement KPIs are measured across supply categories, buyer segments, and more. This metric will help businesses ensure whether suppliers are delivering what was ordered and if it was delivered at the right time. Indicators to track:"

Título: "2.1 RATE OF EMERGENCY PURCHASES". Texto: "Emergency purchases are those unplanned orders which are acquired to prevent the shortage of products/services. This metric is measured with the ratio of emergency purchases to the total number of purchases over a fixed period of time. By lowering the rate of emergency orders, organizations can:"

## Slide 12

Título: "2.2 SUPPLIER LEAD TIME". Texto: "Supplier lead time is the amount of time that elapses between the time a supplier receives an order and the time when the order is shipped. This KPI is often measured in days. Vendor lead time starts with availability confirmation and ordering and ends with the delivery of goods."

Fórmula (viñeta amarilla):
$$\text{Supplier lead time} = \text{Delivery time (Goods and receipts delivery)} - \text{Order time (PO acceptance)}$$

Foto decorativa a la derecha: mujer de oficina volteando un reloj de arena sobre su escritorio, con laptop y gafas al fondo — simboliza el paso del tiempo/lead time.

## Slide 13

Título: "2.3 PURCHASE ORDER CYCLE TIME". Texto: "Purchase order cycle time is measured in hours or days from the time a purchase requisition (enlace) is submitted to the time when it is transmitted to a vendor or contractor. This KPI covers the end-to-end ordering process which makes up the whole purchase order cycle (enlace)."

Diagrama/ilustración a la derecha: bloques de madera tipo rompecabezas con engranajes grabados y una flecha circular (ciclo) apuntando hacia un reloj — representa el ciclo repetitivo/circular del proceso de PO (purchase order cycle).

## Slide 14

Título: "2.4 VENDOR AVAILABILITY". Texto: "Vendor availability is used to measure a vendor's capacity to respond to emergency demands. This procurement KPI helps organizations determine the degree of reliability they can place on a vendor. Vendor availability (%) is measured by the ratio of the number of time items available on a vendor's side to the number of orders placed with the supplier."

## Slide 17

Título: "3.2 SPEND UNDER MANAGEMENT". Texto: "Spend under management is the percentage of procurement spend that is regulated or controlled by the management. As an organization's spend under management rises up, its ability to optimize cost and forecast expense increases with it."

Fórmula (viñeta amarilla):
$$\text{SUM} = \text{Total approved spend (i.e., direct, indirect, and service-related cost)} - \text{Maverick spend}$$

Ilustración decorativa a la derecha: hombre de traje con lupa gigante observando/vigilando de cerca a otro empleado sentado trabajando en su escritorio — simboliza el control/supervisión del gasto.

## Slide 18

Título: "3.3 PROCUREMENT ROI". Texto: "Procurement ROI is used to determine the profitability and cost-effectiveness of the procurement investment. This metric is best suited for internal analysis."

Fórmula (viñeta amarilla):
$$\text{Procurement ROI} = \frac{\text{Annual cost savings}}{\text{Annual procurement cost}}$$

Ilustración decorativa a la derecha: chanchito alcancía (piggy bank) rosado con gafas de lectura junto a una calculadora — simboliza ahorro/análisis financiero.
