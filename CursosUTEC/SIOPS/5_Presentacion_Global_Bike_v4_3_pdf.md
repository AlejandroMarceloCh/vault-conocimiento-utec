## Slide 6 — Global Bike Group

Texto solo, sin diagramas.

- **Background**:
  - Global concern using full ERP capabilities
  - Consists of two companies located in the US and in Germany
- **History**:
  - John Davis won numerous mountain bike championships in the US
  - In 1990, started his own mountain bike company (Frankenstein Bikes)
  - Peter Schwarz grew up on road bikes in the Black Forest, Germany
  - As a student, he engineered ultra-light composite frames
  - In 1993, started his own bike frame company (Heidelberg Composites)
  - Both met in 2000
  - In 2001, merged to form Global Bike Inc.

## Slide 7 — Global Bike Group (Strategy)

Texto solo, sin diagramas.

- **Strategy**:
  - John and Peter are Co-CEOs
  - John is responsible for (in terms of reporting): Sales, Marketing, Service and Support, IT, Finance, and Human Resources — sells products and brings in money
  - Peter is responsible for: Research, Design, Procurement, and Manufacturing Groups — builds products and spends money
  - World-class bicycle company serving both the professional and "prosumer" in touring and off-road racing.
  - Focus on: Quality, Toughness, Performance

## Slide 19 — Process Integration (Example) — Order-to-Cash

- **Order-to-Cash Process**: Sales and Distribution (SD), Materials Management (MM), Financial Accounting (FI)

Diagrama de flujo de proceso cross-modulo tipo "swimlane" horizontal con carriles SD / MM / FI (etiquetas de modulo a la izquierda) y una secuencia de pasos representados como flechas/rombos en 3D apuntando en diagonal, cada uno con el nombre de la transaccion, conectados por lineas punteadas horizontales que indican el carril/modulo responsable de cada paso.

**Primer flujo (parte superior, carril SD activo):**
Create New Customer → Create Contact Person for Customer → Change Customer → Create Customer Inquiry → Create Customer Quotation → Create Sales Order Referencing a Quotation → Check Stock Status (en amarillo/MM) → Display Sales Order → Start Delivery Process → Check Stock Status (en amarillo/MM)

**Segundo flujo (parte inferior, continuacion):**
Pick Materials on Delivery Note (SD) → Post Goods Issue (MM) → Check Stock Status (MM) → Create Invoice for Customer (FI, en verde) → Display Billing Doc. and Customer Invoice (FI) → Post Receipt of Customer Payment (FI) → Review Document Flow (SD)

Cada paso esta codificado por color segun el modulo responsable: azul = SD, amarillo/naranja = MM, verde = FI.

## Slide 20 — Process Integration (Example) — Procure-to-Cash

- **Procure-to-Cash Process**: Materials Management (MM), Financial Accounting (FI)

Diagrama similar al de la slide 19, tipo swimlane con carriles MM / FI, pasos en flechas 3D diagonales conectados por lineas punteadas horizontales.

**Primer flujo (parte superior):**
Create New Vendor → Create Material Master for Trading Goods → Extend Material Master for Trading Goods → Display Stock/Requirements List → Create Purchase Requisition → Display Stock/Requirements List → Create Request for Quotation → Maintain Quotation from Vendors → Evaluate Quotations on Price → Reject Quotations

**Segundo flujo (parte inferior):**
Create Purchase Order Referencing an RFQ → Display Purchase Order → Create Goods Receipt for Purchase Order → Verify Physical Receipt of Goods → Create Invoice Receipt from Vendor (FI, verde) → Create Goods Receipt for Purchase Order → Create Invoice Receipt from Vendor (FI) → Post Payments to Vendor (FI) → Display Vendor Line Items → Display G/L Account Balances and Line Items (FI)

Codificacion de color por modulo: azul = MM, verde = FI.
